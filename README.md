This repository contains my work completed as part of the Infosys Springboard Artificial Intelligence Virtual Internship.

The internship focused on designing and developing an AI-powered Event Management Platform capable of supporting event organizers through intelligent agents, automation, analytics, dashboards, real-time monitoring, and decision support.

The internship was completed through four milestones covering different aspects of event management and enterprise deployment.

🚀 Project Overview

The platform is designed to improve event management by using Artificial Intelligence, automation, analytics, and agent-based workflows.

The four major modules developed during the internship are:

Registration Intelligence & Attendee Management
Venue & Speaker Operations
Sponsorship & Incident Management
Event Intelligence & Enterprise Deployment
